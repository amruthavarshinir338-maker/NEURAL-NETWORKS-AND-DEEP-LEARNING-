# Artificial Neural Network (ANN) – MSc Data Science Study Notes

Welcome to the **Artificial Neural Network (ANN) Study Materials Repository** for MSc Data Science students.

This repository contains structured study notes covering the fundamental concepts, architectures, learning algorithms and different models of Artificial Neural Networks. It is designed to support classroom learning, self-study and university examination preparation.

## 📚 Course Information

- **Programme:** MSc Data Science
- **Subject:** Artificial Neural Networks (ANN)
- **Material Type:** Study Notes and Learning Resources
- **Target Audience:** MSc Data Science Students

## 🎯 Learning Objectives

After studying these materials, students will be able to:

- Understand the fundamentals and architecture of Artificial Neural Networks.
- Explain the structure and functioning of artificial neurons.
- Understand different ANN models and their characteristics.
- Describe various learning algorithms and weight adjustment techniques.
- Differentiate between feedforward and recurrent neural networks.
- Understand the applications, advantages and limitations of ANN models.
